# CFD commodity marks

VOLTEX's own drawings for this terminal (2026-10-15), generated by
`scripts/cfd-marks/generate.py` — no third-party artwork, no licence needed.

- `gold.svg`, `silver.svg`, `platinum.svg`, `palladium.svg` — a stack of three ingots in the metal's colour.
- `wti.svg`, `brent.svg` — a crude-oil drop (amber disc for WTI, blue disc for Brent).

Each mark is shown with the quote currency's flag (`../cfd-flags`, flag-icons, MIT) over its corner,
the way exchanges and TradingView show a commodity pair.
